Privacy policy

Mission Vibe Backup is operated for its owner's personal use. It reads selected local folders and uses Google Drive access to create, read, update and remove the backup files it manages. Restic encrypts the backup data before uploading it.

The app stores Google authorization credentials on the owner's computer to run scheduled backups. Its owner can revoke the app's Google account access in Google Account settings. Backup data remains in Google Drive until the owner removes it.

The app does not sell backup data or use it for advertising. Google Drive and GitHub Pages operate under their own privacy policies. This public information page does not contain backup data, access credentials or encryption passwords.
